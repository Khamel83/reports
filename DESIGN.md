# Design

Warm paper background, dark ink, one terracotta accent, serif headings, sans body, 760 px column.
Light and dark follow the system. Print hides reply controls. Everything comes from `tokens.css`; do not add colours.

Parts: `.kicker` (repo · ticket · date), `h1`, `.lede` (the answer in one sentence), `h2` sections,
`.card` and `.card.verdict` (the recommendation), `table` for comparisons, `.tag` with `.good` `.warn` `.bad`,
`.note` for caveats, `.meta` footer (author, date, what is unverified).

Write order: answer first, then evidence, then what to do next. Short sentences. No filler.
